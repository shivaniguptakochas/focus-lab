📐 Focus Score Calculator Formula

FocusLab calculates a transparent score from 0–100 using study compliance and distraction penalties:

Focus Score = Clamp [ ((Actual Duration / Planned Duration) × 100) - (Distractions × 6) + Completion Bonus (5), 0, 100 ]

Academic Notice: This score serves as an operational heuristic for self-monitoring study session quality and is not a clinically validated psychological diagnostic instrument.

BU100 Reading Connections & Research Methodology

University Experimental Research Framework

This research project was designed for Bennett University under the BU100 SDG Action Lab (TREP) End-Term Project, addressing SDG 4 (Quality Education) and SDG 3 (Good Health and Well-Being).

1. The Scout Mindset — Julia Galef

In *The Scout Mindset*, Julia Galef distinguishes between the "Soldier Mindset" (motivated reasoning, defending prior assumptions) and the "Scout Mindset" (seeking an accurate map of reality). In FocusLab, we resist the soldier urge to guarantee that "phone-free study must always win." Instead, we report real differences honestly, balance condition orders, and acknowledge cases where phone checks had minimal impact.

2. Calling Bullshit — Carl T. Bergstrom & Jevin D. West

Bergstrom and West argue that statistical charts and metrics can be manipulated to produce misleading claims. To combat data distortions in FocusLab:
• Every chart starts its vertical axis from zero to prevent exaggerated visual slope differences.
• We explicitly state that an $N=10$ student sample provides preliminary educational insights, not population-level causality.
• Missing participant data is transparently flagged rather than interpolated.

3. Thinking, Fast and Slow — Daniel Kahneman

Kahneman’s framework explains the cognitive cost of smartphones:
• System 1: Fast, automatic dopamine impulses prompt reflexive smartphone glances whenever a screen lights up.
• System 2: Deliberate, analytical comprehension demands intense metabolic energy.
When students keep a phone visible, System 2 must expend precious executive energy continuously suppressing System 1 impulses, causing "cognitive drain."

Experimental Counterbalancing Protocol

To mitigate order effects and fatigue effects, participants alternate condition order (Participant P01 completes Condition A first, P02 completes Condition B first, and so forth). Furthermore, the two standardized reading passages (Passage 1: Cognitive Load Theory; Passage 2: Neuroscience of Habit Loops) and their 10-question quizzes are rotated to ensure passage familiarity does not bias test scores.

FocusLab User Guide & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does FocusLab require an internet connection?

A: No! FocusLab uses browser LocalStorage and procedural Web Audio API synthesis for ambient sound. It functions completely offline once loaded.

Q: How is my Focus Score calculated?

A: It calculates duration compliance (ratio of completed study time to planned time), deducts 6 points per logged interruption, and awards a +5 point bonus for finishing without aborting.

Q: How do I export experimental data for my research report?

A: Navigate to the Experiment Lab and click "Export CSV Dataset". This generates an RFC4180-compliant CSV spreadsheet of all participant trials.

Q: Can I use FocusLab on my phone?

A: Yes! FocusLab features a fully responsive mobile layout with a collapsible drawer navigation sidebar.
